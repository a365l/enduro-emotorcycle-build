- **Phase 1 - donor bikes & repairs:** bought two crashed donor ebikes, stripped them for parts and sold the rest. Bought a broken motor and battery to repair and resell. Diagnosed a secondhand Sabvaton controller with shorted MOSFETs and attempted a repair. ([phase 1](../../phase-1-donor-bikes-and-repairs/README.md))
- **Phase 2 - QS205 + Fardriver:** fitted a QS205 hub motor on a 19" rim and a Fardriver ND72450 controller, rewired the whole loom with crimped terminals, built a Surron-to-Fardriver throttle adapter, ran autolearn and bench-tested. ([phase 2](../../phase-2-fardriver/README.md))
- Hydraulic brake bleed, Fardriver parameter setup, and the engineering docs in `00`-`08`.
